HEADLINE / ANSWER
================================================================================
* lost-lock stayed 0.00% on both clips with masking on -- masking does not hurt
  lock, and there are still no cuts.

* The answer is CLIP-DEPENDENT -- it is BOTH causes, in different proportions:
    - When players CLUSTER in view (huddles, congestion) and dominate the frame,
      most of the unmasked drift was the no-masking shortcut. Masking LARGELY
      FIXES it.  (Loveland: ~300 px -> ~30 px.)
    - When players are DISPERSED and the drift comes from hard zoom over low-
      texture floor, masking BARELY HELPS -- it's a real tracking limit.
      (Princeton: stays ~135-150 px regardless.)

* So masking is necessary and clearly worth keeping (it already is, in the real
  pipeline), and it rescues the player-congestion failure mode. But it is NOT a
  complete fix: a residual ~135-150 px drift over 90 s remains on at least one
  Tactical clip, and that would still make full-game team stats progressively
  wrong on that footage.
