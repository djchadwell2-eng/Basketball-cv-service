Stage 5 -- both-basket court overlay (global H_court + SIGN FIX)

ROOT CAUSE (revised): the Stage-4 "blank right half" was NOT a single-reference
representation limit. It was a homography SIGN-convention bug. A homography is
defined only up to sign; for right-basket views findHomography returned the
transform with the sign flipped, so the visible court points came out with w<0
and the draw guard (w<=0 -> skip) dropped them -- even though they divide to the
CORRECT pixels. Sign-normalizing the court->frame transform (flip so a central
court point (42,25) has w>0) renders both baskets from the existing GLOBAL
H_court. The per-view rework was therefore NOT built -- it was unnecessary.
